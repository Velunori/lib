# Omarchy

`kit/Panel.qml` is adapted from Omarchy 4.0.6-1's `shell/Ui/KeyboardPanel.qml`.
The shared components were extracted from Velunori core revision
`d5e8270b4c96b8e4b02f62762deb11a036cba739`.

those modules remain owned by Omarchy. It does not ship a shell, bar, daemon
or authentication implementation.
