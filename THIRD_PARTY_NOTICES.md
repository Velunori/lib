# Omarchy

`Bar.qml` is adapted from `/usr/share/omarchy/shell/plugins/bar/Bar.qml` in
the installed Omarchy **4.0.6-1** package. `BarModel.js` is copied from the
same directory. Upstream: https://github.com/basecamp/omarchy.

`kit/Panel.qml` is adapted from `shell/Ui/KeyboardPanel.qml` in that package.
